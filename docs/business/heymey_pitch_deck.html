Who we serve

An aspirant studying alone, on a phone, with no clear compass.

  • Studies 2–4 hours a day, mostly alone
  • Often a repeat attempter

Retention by design

A daily loop built to be kept, not feared.

Designed against guilt

  • A monthly streak shield quietly forgives one missed day.
  • Calm notifications name your real weak topic. No generic "study now".
  • Pause without penalty. Return to exactly where you left off.
  • A weekly letter from Sudi explains your week in plain words.

Sudi is a small flame character. It appears only at moments that matter: wins, empty states and loading.

Why we size from the bottom up

Published estimates for India's online test-prep market range from about USD 11 million to over USD 11 billion, depending on how the market is defined. Official turnout figures are firmer ground.

Why mobile-first is the right bet

India has 958 million active internet users, and 57% of them are in rural areas (IAMAI–Kantar, 2025).

How the market widens

Police recruitment (3,644 constable and 1,299 sub-inspector posts in 2025), teacher eligibility, banking, SSC and railway exams.

Go-to-market

Discipline on acquisition cost

  • Educators earn a share only when a sale happens.
  • Referral rewards are credits first, cash only on a first purchase.
  • Any channel above about ₹250 per paying user is stopped.
