A shared target — "relight 3,000 lamps together this month." Unlock squad banners and cosmetics when you hit it.

⚔️ Squad Battle (twice a week)

  • Two squads of similar strength matched
  • 10 topic zones from the shared syllabus
  • Each member gets 2 attempts — timed 5-question sets
  • 3 correct = 1 flag · 4 = 2 flags · 5 = 3 flags
  • Most flags wins — Rewards: Points, squad XP, Trophies

🔒 Privacy first

  • Leaderboards: see top ranks + your own. Hide yourself anytime.
  • No phone numbers or links in squad chat
